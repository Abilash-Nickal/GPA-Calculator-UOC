DATA IMPORT
Import from University Portal

Copy your results directly from the SIS portal and paste them in — the app handles the rest.

1Open your Result Sheet in the UOC student portal.
2Select all and copy the results table.
3Navigate to INPUT RESULTS in the sidebar.
4Paste the text and tap PROCESS DATA.

Step 1 — Copy results from the University Portal

Step 2 — Paste into the Input Results section

INSTALL AS APP (PWA)
Use as a Mobile App — No Download Needed

Add the tracker to your home screen for a native app experience.

IPHONE (SAFARI)
1Open Safari and visit the tracker URL.
2Tap the Share button (square with arrow up).
3Scroll down and tap "Add to Home Screen".
4Tap "Add" in the top right.
ANDROID (CHROME)
1Open Chrome and visit the tracker URL.
2Tap the Menu (three dots ⋮).
3Tap "Add to Home screen" or "Install App".
4Tap "Add" to confirm.
